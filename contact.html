Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our products, shipping, and natural healing approaches.

How do I know which herbs are right for my condition?

Our certified herbalists provide free personalized consultations via WhatsApp or phone. We'll assess your health goals, current conditions, and any medications you're taking to recommend the most appropriate herbal remedies. Every consultation is confidential and tailored to your specific needs.

Are your herbal remedies safe to use with prescription medications?

Safety is our top priority. During your consultation, we'll review all medications and supplements you're currently taking. Our herbalists are trained to identify potential interactions and will only recommend herbs that are safe for your specific situation. Always consult with your healthcare provider before starting any new supplement regimen.

How long does it take to see results from herbal remedies?

Results vary depending on the individual, condition, and specific herbs being used. Some people notice improvements within a few days, while others may take 2-4 weeks to experience significant benefits. Our herbalists will provide realistic expectations and monitor your progress throughout your healing journey.

What is your return policy if the products don't work for me?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all our products. If you're not completely satisfied with your results, simply return the unused portion within 30 days for a full refund. We also provide ongoing support to adjust your regimen if needed - sometimes a different combination of herbs may work better for your unique situation.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping typically takes 7-14 business days depending on your location. Some countries may have restrictions on certain herbal products, so please check with us if you have any concerns about customs or import regulations in your country.

How do I place an order through WhatsApp?

Ordering through WhatsApp is simple! Just start a chat with us, tell us which products you'd like to order, and we'll guide you through the process. We accept various payment methods including bank transfer, mobile money, and credit cards. We'll confirm your order details and provide tracking information once your package ships.
